Privacy

Effective 19 September 2026

Summary

Hronir LLC collects as little personal information as it can manage. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

This website

This site is a set of static files. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub processes standard server request data — including IP address, user agent, and requested URL — in order to serve pages and protect against abuse. This is handled under GitHub's own privacy practices and we do not receive or retain those logs.

Fonts

The site loads a typeface from Google Fonts. Your browser requests that file directly from Google, which means Google receives your IP address as part of the request. No cookie is set by that request.

If you email us

When you write to hello@hronir.dev, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you attach. We use it to answer you and to keep a record of the correspondence. We retain support email for as long as it is useful and then delete it.

Our applications

Where a Hronir LLC application collects personal information, the specifics are disclosed in that application and in its App Store privacy label. Our general practice is to keep data on your device, to avoid collecting what we do not need, and to ask before transmitting anything.

Where an application requires an account or transmits data to a server, its own documentation will describe what is sent, why, and how long it is kept.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, to ask us to correct or delete it, and to object to certain processing. Residents of California, the European Economic Area, and the United Kingdom have specific statutory rights of this kind.

To make a request, email hello@hronir.dev. We will respond within the period required by applicable law. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights.

Children

Our website and applications are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and, where the change is significant, note it on this page.
