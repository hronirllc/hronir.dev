What we do

Hronir LLC builds iOS applications, with a focus on data organization — tools for structuring, searching, and keeping track of information that would otherwise pile up.

Our apps are local-only: your data and all the processing stay on your device. It's your data, so you should control it. We support iCloud backups because they go through Apple at your discretion and we never see them. We don't have a cloud and we don't charge subscriptions. We sell you an app and then it's yours.
